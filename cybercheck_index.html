About CyberCheck

CyberCheck is designed as an accessible cybersecurity education and utility platform. The goal is to make useful security concepts understandable without overwhelming beginners.

Our tools are intended for defensive and educational use. Results are indicators, not guarantees. A URL can look normal and still be malicious, and a strong password score cannot guarantee account security.

Never paste private keys, recovery phrases, authentication codes, or other secrets into third-party websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CyberCheck store my password?

The V1 password analyzer processes the value in your browser and does not send it to a server. For maximum safety, do not use a real password while experimenting; test with a representative sample.

Can the URL Inspector prove a website is safe?

No. It checks visible technical indicators and suspicious patterns. It cannot guarantee that a website is legitimate.

Is CyberCheck an antivirus?

No. It is a cybersecurity toolkit and educational platform, not an antivirus, EDR or malware-removal product.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the client-side V1 tools.

Can I use CyberCheck on mobile?

Yes. The interface is responsive and designed for mobile browsers.

Can CyberCheck check leaked passwords?

Not in this offline V1. A production breach-check feature should use a reputable privacy-preserving service or k-anonymity approach rather than transmitting plaintext passwords.
